A sweet stop, a bigger conversation

The Jon Cake
phenomenon.

Jon García’s cheesecake shop helped turn a local dessert into a destination. The first small Born shop, social-media momentum, queues, and later expansion made the cake part of the neighborhood’s visitor story.

Some say “John Cake,” but the shop is Jon Cake. Local reporting documents visitors from China, Poland, the United States, Korea, and Japan, with some travelers discovering the shop on social platforms in their home markets. The story reflects how a very local pastry can travel through global food culture. We’re describing a reported trend, not guessing anyone’s identity. Taste the cake; be kind to the neighbors; keep the pavement clear.

The long view

A thousand years of making room.

Sant Pere de les Puel·les began as a monastery. The neighborhood kept changing—crafts, water, mills, factories, migration, markets, art. The story is still being written by the people who live here.
