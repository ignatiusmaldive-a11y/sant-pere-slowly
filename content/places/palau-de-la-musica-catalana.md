The Palau de la Música Catalana is a concert hall by architect Lluís Domènech i Montaner and a landmark of Catalan Modernisme. Its bright interiors are best experienced on a concert visit or an official guided tour; from the street, approach along Sant Pere Més Alt and look for the sculptural façade details.

Book through the venue for current tours, concerts and access information. The Palau is a major destination, but it sits close to the smaller, quieter streets of Sant Pere.
