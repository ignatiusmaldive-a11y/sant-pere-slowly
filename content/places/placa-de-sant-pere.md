Plaça de Sant Pere is a natural pause in a walk through the quarter. The church of Sant Pere de les Puel·les anchors the square; sit for a moment and notice how the traffic of nearby streets softens here.

This is an everyday square as well as a historic setting. Keep paths and entrances clear, and take the time to look at the church from more than one side.
