RAI (Recursos d’Animació Intercultural) is a community cultural association on Carrer dels Carders. Its activity spans performances, workshops and participatory arts; the program is the best reason to visit, and it changes regularly.

Look at the calendar before you go, especially if you are planning around a particular show or class. The venue is part of a lived-in street, so arrive quietly and respect the neighbors.

**Nearby:** [Capella d’en Marcús](/sant-pere-slowly/places/capella-den-marcus/), [Solar Benet Corner](/sant-pere-slowly/places/solar-benet-corner/).
