Jon Cake’s cheesecake became part of Barcelona’s visitor conversation through social video, food coverage and word of mouth. Local reporting has described visitors arriving from China, Korea and Japan alongside travelers from many other places. It offers a useful glimpse of how food tourism moves: a small bakery can become a stop on an international itinerary.

Enjoy the cake, leave the pavement clear for neighbors, and check the shop’s current address and hours before making a special trip. The phenomenon is about food, platforms and travel habits—not assumptions about any individual visitor.
