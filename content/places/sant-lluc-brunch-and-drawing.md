Cercle Artístic de Sant Lluc was founded in 1893 and has long offered space for artists to study and practice. Today, visitors can look for drawing and painting activities and settle in at Lluc Brunx, the café. It makes a particularly good slower stop: a sketchbook, a coffee and a little time indoors.

Check the association and café pages for current access, activities and service hours. Classes and studio use may require advance booking.
