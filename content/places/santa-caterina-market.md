Santa Caterina is a good place to begin because it is still a market first: arrive in the morning, follow the produce counters, and take time to look up at the roof. The current market was reshaped by architects Enric Miralles and Benedetta Tagliabue; the lively roof brings color into the everyday work of shopping.

For a sit-down pause, **Bar Joan** is inside the market. Keep the market’s working rhythm in mind: leave room for shoppers and check opening times before you come.

**Look for:** the shifting roof from the surrounding streets, the stalls waking up, and the market bar counters.
