Known locally as La Bonne, the Francesca Bonnemaison space grew from the school and women’s cultural institute founded by Francesca Bonnemaison in 1909. It remains a place for learning and public life, with a library and a program of activities.

The doorway and threshold are worth noticing, but this is a working public institution: check opening hours and visitor rules before you plan a stop.
