Bar Joan is part of the market’s daily life: a compact counter and a place to pause over a Catalan-style breakfast or lunch. It sits inside Santa Caterina Market at stalls 108–110. The useful thing here is the setting as much as the plate: you are eating in the middle of a working market, with the day still unfolding around you.

Go at a relaxed pace, ask what is good that day, and make space for people who are shopping or working. Check the market’s current listing for hours before visiting.
