Solar Benet Corner is a neighborhood-scale pause near Passatge de Sant Benet, best experienced as part of a slow walk between Plaça de Sant Pere and Basses de Sant Pere. It is a modest spot rather than a destination venue; the interest is in how small open spaces sit among the dense streets of the old city.

The name is used here as a map reference for the corner. Check the map pin for its position and be considerate of homes and doorways around it.

**Nearby:** [Plaça de Sant Pere](/sant-pere-slowly/places/placa-de-sant-pere/), [Basses de Sant Pere](/sant-pere-slowly/places/basses-de-sant-pere/).
